Rules

The rule changed in July

A fair-lending audit measures outcomes. Whether a gap creates legal risk depends on the rule, and one US rule changed in 2026.

April 22, 2026The CFPB issues a final rule amending Regulation B, which implements ECOA.
July 21, 2026The rule takes effect. It removes the “effects test” and states that ECOA does not authorize disparate-impact liability.
UnchangedDisparate treatment and the intentional use of proxies for protected traits stay prohibited, according to law-firm summaries. The Fair Housing Act and state laws did not change, and a court challenge was filed.
Sources: Federal Register 2026-07804; summaries from Cooley, Venable and Husch Blackwell. A student project, not legal advice.

We test for proxies as well as gaps. A model with the protected columns removed can still rebuild them from other columns.
