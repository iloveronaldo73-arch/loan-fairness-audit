03Check

How much could this applicant borrow?

Enter an applicant and a loan. The tool estimates the largest loan the income can carry, and looks up the credit file.

Demo only. Use made-up details. Name, address and telephone are not used by any model and never leave your browser. The share of income and the interest rate are assumptions you can change, not rules.

In a real deployment the credit history would come from a credit bureau, fetched by a backend with the applicant’s consent. This demo has no bureau access, so you choose a synthetic profile. Years of credit history, open credit lines and credit utilization are not inputs.
